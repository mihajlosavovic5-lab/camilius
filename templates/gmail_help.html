How to connect Gmail (4 steps, ~2 minutes)

ai-auto-reply reads your email using what's called an "app password" — a special password Google generates just for this purpose, separate from your regular Gmail password. Your regular password is never entered anywhere.

1

Enable 2-Step Verification

Google requires 2-step verification to be enabled on your account before it will allow an app password. If you already have it enabled, skip to step 2.

Open Google settings →

2

Create an App Password

On the page that opens, enter a name (e.g. "ai-auto-reply") and click "Create". Google will show you 16 characters — that's your app password.

Open App Passwords →

3

Enable IMAP

In Gmail: gear icon (top right) → "See all settings" → the "Forwarding and POP/IMAP" tab → enable "Enable IMAP" → save changes ("Save Changes" at the bottom).

Open Gmail settings →

4

Enter the details in registration

Go back to registration, enter your Gmail address, and paste in that 16-character app password (spaces in it don't matter, you can leave them in).

Back to registration

Delete Your Account

Option 1: Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Log in to your Camilius account.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the "Danger Zone" section and click Delete Account.

This is immediate and permanent - there is no recovery period.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't log in, send a deletion request from your account's contact email to support@ai-auto-reply-mail.com, including your business/username. We'll confirm and delete the account manually.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, immediately:

  • Your business profile (name, username, contact email, password, working hours, price list, FAQ)
  • Your connected Instagram account link and access token
  • All conversations and messages (customer questions, suggested and sent replies)
  • Appointments, push notification tokens, and usage/error logs tied to your account

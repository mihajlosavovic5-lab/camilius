Common questions
Why didn't a customer message show up?
Make sure your page is published in Profile, and that your daily reply limit hasn't been reached.
Can I see what the AI told a customer?
Yes - every reply is sent instantly since the customer is chatting live, and you can review the full conversation anytime in Conversations.
How does the AI know what to reply with?
It uses the working hours, price list, and FAQ answers saved in your Profile, plus OpenAI, to draft a reply suited to each customer message.
Can Camilius book appointments automatically?
Yes - when a customer asks for a time that fits your working hours, it can book the appointment directly and let you know.
How do no-show reminders work?
Turn them on in Settings to get an app notification a set amount of time before each upcoming appointment.
Can I change my working hours or prices later?
Yes - update them anytime in Profile. The AI always uses whatever is saved there for new replies.
What's included in the Pro plan?
Pro unlocks features like no-show reminders. Check Subscription in Settings for the current plan and pricing.
Is my data shared with anyone?
Customer messages and your business info are sent to OpenAI to generate replies. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes - go to Settings and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and conversations and can't be undone.

Common questions
Why didn't a customer message show up?
Make sure your Instagram account is still connected in Profile, and that it's a Professional (Business or Creator) account - that's required for Camilius to read its messages.
Can I edit a reply before sending?
Yes - every AI-drafted reply is just a suggestion. Open it from the inbox, edit the text if you like, then send.
How does the AI know what to reply with?
It uses the working hours, price list, and FAQ answers saved in your Profile, plus OpenAI, to draft a reply suited to each customer message.
Do I need a Professional Instagram account?
Yes - a Business or Creator account is required. Personal accounts can't grant the access Camilius needs to read and send messages.
Can Camilius book appointments automatically?
Yes - when a customer asks for a time that fits your working hours, it can book the appointment directly and let you know.
What does AI Draft Mode do?
When it's on, every AI-suggested reply waits in your inbox for you to review, edit, and send yourself instead of sending automatically.
How do no-show reminders work?
Turn them on in Settings to notify the customer, yourself, or both before an appointment. Customer reminders go by Instagram DM; yours arrive as an app notification.
Can I change my working hours or prices later?
Yes - update them anytime in Profile. The AI always uses whatever is saved there for new replies.
What's included in the Pro plan?
Pro unlocks features like no-show reminders. Check Subscription in Settings for the current plan and pricing.
Is my data shared with anyone?
Customer messages and your business info are sent to OpenAI to generate replies. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes - go to Settings and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and conversations and can't be undone.
Why did a conversation get archived as expired?
Instagram only allows replying within 24 hours of the customer's last message, so conversations past that window are archived automatically.
